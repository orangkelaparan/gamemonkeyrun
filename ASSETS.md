# Jungle Monkey Run Assets

**Art direction:** Premium stylized 2D jungle illustration with subtle realism. The play space uses pale, misty green depth in the distance and saturated deep jungle greens in the foreground. The brown monkey and warm gold / yellow collectibles are deliberately high contrast against the greenery. Shapes have clean dark edges, gentle gloss highlights, and family-friendly personality.
